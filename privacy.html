RIPPL Studio Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

RIPPL Studio is an interactive fluid simulation, available as an Android app and as a web page. This policy covers both.

The short version

RIPPL Studio does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it works without an internet connection.

What stays on your device

  • Presets and settings. The looks you save and your app settings are stored only on your device. Removing the app, or clearing the site data in your browser, deletes them.
  • Images. When you tap Capture, the image is saved to your device’s gallery (in “Pictures/RIPPL Studio”) or handed to the app you choose in the share sheet. RIPPL Studio never reads your existing photos or files, apart from a preset file you choose to import.

Permissions

The Android app asks for one permission: the microphone, and only if you switch on “Sound reactive” (Scene → Reacts to). The fluid follows the loudness and pitch of what it hears; no sound is recorded, stored or sent anywhere, and the microphone is released as soon as the feature is off or the app is out of sight. On the live wallpaper the microphone is only used if you also allow it in System → App. “Tilt” and “Shake” read the phone’s motion sensor, which needs no permission. The app also uses vibration for touch feedback, which you can switch off in System → App.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your device unless you share it yourself, for example by sending a preset or an image through the share sheet. What happens next is covered by the privacy policy of the app you shared it with.

The web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address when the page is loaded; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. RIPPL Studio itself sets no cookies.

Children

RIPPL Studio is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Questions are welcome through the project’s issue tracker.
